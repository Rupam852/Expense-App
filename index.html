Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about Grow Expense App.

Is my financial data shared with anyone?

+

No. Grow Expense stores all statement rows and manual entries on a secure offline database inside your phone's local sandbox. Cloud sync to our backup server is completely optional and can be managed directly from user settings.

How does the AI statement scanning work?

+

When parsing statements or scanning receipts, the app sends the document or image to our secure cloud gateway, which uses advanced AI models to extract key fields. You can also configure a custom AI API Key in the app preferences to run calls on your own quota.

Is the Biometric lock secure?

+

Yes. The app utilizes Android's native Biometric API. All authentication flows are verified in the device's secure enclave. Grow Expense never accesses, views, or stores your biometric records.

Can I back up my information?

+

Yes. You can optionally enable private Supabase Cloud Sync inside the app. This backs up and synchronizes all your Expenses, Budgets, Payment accounts, Khata entries, Subscriptions, and Split bills across devices seamlessly.

How does Khata / Udhar Book & WhatsApp Reminders work?

+

The Khata screen lets you log who you lent money to or borrowed from. You can record due dates, partial payments, and tap the WhatsApp icon to automatically generate a friendly reminder message containing your direct UPI payment link.

How does Split Bills work with friends?

+

You can create a Split Bill for group dinners, road trips, or flat rent. The app calculates each person's exact share, tracks who has settled, and allows you to share payment links directly over WhatsApp.

How do Voice Entry & SMS Parsing work?

+

You can speak naturally into the app or paste standard bank debit SMS texts. The intelligent parsing engine detects the amount, merchant name, category, and payment channel with zero manual typing.

How do in-app OTA updates work?

+

Whenever a new version is released on GitHub, the app alerts you with a changelog badge in Settings. You can download and install new versions directly inside the app without needing manual browser searches.

What is the Month Rollover clean-up and does it delete my data?

+

At the end of each month, the app prompts you with a Rollover window. It summarizes your total monthly expenses and allows you to compile all transactions into a structured PDF statement directly inside your device's Downloads directory. Once compiled, you can clean up the local database to keep the app fast and clutter-free for the new month.

Privacy Policy for Grow Expense App

Last Updated: June 2026

1. Information Collection and Use

Grow Expense App is designed as a **local-first** application. This means all personal financial data, budgets, accounts, and transaction history are stored securely on your device's local database. We do not transmit or sell your personal data to external databases or third parties.

2. Statement Scanning and AI Parsing

When you use the UPI statement parsing feature, the app processes the file contents. If you choose to parse via AI features, the file details are passed directly to our secure AI models using the API Key you provide. This transaction is encrypted and communicates directly with Google. No intermediary servers store or process your statements.

3. Device Permissions

  • Storage / Files: Used solely to pick statement documents for parsing, and to save generated PDF and CSV logs to your device's Downloads directory.
  • Biometrics / Device Lock: Used to confirm identity when launching the app. The biometric authentication is handled entirely by your phone's native operating system (Android BiometricPrompt). The app never sees, stores, or accesses your actual fingerprint or passcode records.

4. Optional Cloud Sync

If you choose to enable the optional Private Cloud Backup sync feature, your local database records will sync dynamically to your personal private backup cloud storage. This feature is entirely optional and can be toggled off at any time. Data ownership belongs solely to you.

5. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Since the app is local-first, policy modifications will be published here on the landing page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding privacy, feel free to contact us at growexpensetrackerapp@gmail.com.
