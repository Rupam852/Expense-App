Sleek Features, Absolute Privacy

A full suite of personal accounting tools built for the modern offline-first budget workflow.

Batch Statement Imports (PDF & Excel)

Directly upload large bank statement PDF or Excel logs (including password-protected statement files) from Google Pay, PhonePe, or netbanking. The app automatically processes and maps transaction rows using a secure AI engine, sorting categories and merchants in seconds.

Smart OCR Receipt Scanner

Take photos of bills and receipts. The scanner calls an advanced AI engine to extract items, dates, and payment methods automatically.

Secure Local Database

Your budgets and transaction records remain entirely offline on your phone inside a secure, encrypted sandbox database.

Month Rollover Clean-up

When a new month starts, the app compiles the previous month's total ledger. It lets you compile a detailed expense PDF statement formatted in standard DD/MM/YYYY directly to your Downloads folder before clearing the local database to start fresh.

Ultra-Fast Supabase Sync

Everything runs with zero network latency off a local SQLite database. If you wish to synchronize data across devices, you can execute the fastest ledger synchronization directly to your secure Supabase database, merging data seamlessly in milliseconds.

Interactive Charts & Expense Analytics

Visualize your monthly category outflows. Beautiful, real-time pie charts and dynamic bar graphs help you track spending patterns and locate savings opportunities instantly.

Multi-Wallet & Payment Profiles

Define custom payment sources—such as Cash, Credit Cards, or specific bank accounts. Link expenses directly to payment profiles and maintain clean registers for different transaction channels.

Tactile Haptic Feedback Profiles

Experience physical, responsive actions. The application utilizes targeted haptic vibration cues for key flows—firing light taps on tab changes, standard impacts on saves, and warning double-pulses on data resets.

Secure Supabase Auth & Google Guard

Protect your backup sync streams with secure Supabase Authentication. Log in instantly with your Google account or register via email with a two-phase secure verification guard, keeping your data authorization tokens encrypted client-side.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about Grow Expense App.

Is my financial data shared with anyone?

+

No. Grow Expense stores all statement rows and manual entries on a secure offline database inside your phone's local sandbox. Cloud sync to our backup server is completely optional and can be managed directly from user settings.

How does the AI statement scanning work?

+

When parsing statements or scanning receipts, the app sends the document or image to our secure cloud gateway, which uses advanced AI models to extract key fields. You can also configure a custom AI API Key in the app preferences to run calls on your own quota.

Is the Biometric lock secure?

+

Yes. The app utilizes Android's native Biometric API. All authentication flows are verified in the device's secure enclave. Grow Expense never accesses, views, or stores your biometric records.

Can I back up my information?

+

Yes. You can optionally enable private Supabase Cloud Sync inside the app. This links dynamically to your own secure Supabase database to backup and sync your financial ledger. You can toggle this on or off at any time.

What is the Month Rollover clean-up and does it delete my data?

+

At the end of each month, the app prompts you with a Rollover window. It summarizes your total monthly expenses and allows you to compile all transactions into a structured PDF statement directly inside your device's Downloads directory. Once compiled, you can clean up the local database to keep the app fast and clutter-free for the new month.

Does local deletion sync automatically, and can I recover data?

+

To preserve local performance and privacy, deleting a transaction locally does not immediately auto-sync to the cloud. However, when you perform a manual sync (Backup to Cloud) or a pull-down refresh on the home screen, your local database merges with the cloud database, and any local deletions are propagated, permanently removing them from the cloud as well. If you delete a transaction accidentally, you can recover it by tapping "Restore from Cloud" before you perform a manual sync or refresh.

Privacy Policy for Grow Expense App

Last Updated: June 2026

1. Information Collection and Use

Grow Expense App is designed as a **local-first** application. This means all personal financial data, budgets, accounts, and transaction history are stored securely on your device's local database. We do not transmit or sell your personal data to external databases or third parties.

2. Statement Scanning and AI Parsing

When you use the UPI statement parsing feature, the app processes the file contents. If you choose to parse via AI features, the file details are passed directly to our secure AI models using the API Key you provide. This transaction is encrypted and communicates directly with Google. No intermediary servers store or process your statements.

3. Device Permissions

  • Storage / Files: Used solely to pick statement documents for parsing, and to save generated PDF and CSV logs to your device's Downloads directory.
  • Biometrics / Device Lock: Used to confirm identity when launching the app. The biometric authentication is handled entirely by your phone's native operating system (Android BiometricPrompt). The app never sees, stores, or accesses your actual fingerprint or passcode records.

4. Optional Cloud Sync

If you choose to enable the optional Private Cloud Backup sync feature, your local database records will sync dynamically to your personal private backup cloud storage. This feature is entirely optional and can be toggled off at any time. Data ownership belongs solely to you.

5. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Since the app is local-first, policy modifications will be published here on the landing page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding privacy, feel free to contact us at growexpensetrackerapp@gmail.com.
