Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about Grow Expense App.

Why should I download the Split APK instead of Universal APK?

+

Split APKs are optimized specifically for your phone's processor architecture (ARM64 for almost all modern Android phones). It reduces download size by ~62% (from 72 MB down to just ~27 MB) and speeds up app installation and launch times.

Which AI models does Grow Expense support?

+

Grow Expense includes built-in Google Gemini 2.5 (Flash, Pro, Flash-Lite) with remote fallback, and also allows you to configure your own custom API keys for Groq (Llama 3.3 70B) or DeepSeek (R1, V3). The app includes a 45-second smart cooldown health test button to check endpoint status safely.

How does Payment Cards & Dynamic UPI QR work?

+

You can add your debit, credit, or bank accounts as sleek payment cards. For receiving money or bill sharing, generate a custom dynamic UPI QR code with a pre-filled amount that anyone can scan via GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, or BHIM.

What is the Mandi Voice Calculator?

+

Designed for market traders, shopkeepers, and daily shoppers! Speak item quantities and rates in Hindi or English (e.g. "50 kg aalu at 22 rupee plus 10 kg pyaj at 35") and the app automatically parses the arithmetic, calculates the totals, and logs it directly.

How does the 1-Tap Help & SMTP Issue Reporting work?

+

Whenever an error occurs or if you have suggestions, simply tap "Help / Report" in error popups or Settings. Your message and optional debug details are transmitted straight to developer email (rupambairagiya08@gmail.com) via secure SMTP.

Is my financial data shared with anyone?

+

No. Grow Expense stores all statement rows and manual entries on a secure offline database inside your phone's local sandbox. Cloud sync to our backup server is completely optional and can be managed directly from user settings.

Privacy Policy for Grow Expense App

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Information Collection and Use

Grow Expense App is designed as a **local-first** application. All personal financial data, payment cards, budgets, khata entries, and transaction history are stored securely on your device's local encrypted sandbox. We do not transmit or sell your personal data to external databases or third parties.

2. Multi-Model AI and Scanning

When you use OCR bill scanning, Mandi voice calculation, or AI financial advisor, data is processed directly via Google Gemini, DeepSeek, or Groq using your chosen keys or encrypted remote fallback. No intermediary party stores your financial logs.

3. Device Permissions

  • Storage / Files: Used solely to pick statement documents for parsing, and to save generated PDF/Excel statements to your device's Downloads directory.
  • Biometrics / Device Lock: Used to confirm identity when launching the app. Authentication is handled natively by Android BiometricPrompt.

4. Optional Cloud Sync

If you choose to enable the optional Private Cloud Backup feature, your local database records sync directly to your private Supabase cloud storage.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback, contact developer support directly at rupambairagiya08@gmail.com.
